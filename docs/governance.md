# Privacy and model governance

- Synthetic identifiers are used in the demo.
- Events lacking analytics consent are excluded from behavioral features.
- The score is a prioritization signal, not an automated eligibility decision.
- Contributions, threshold and routing reason are returned for every lead.
- Protected attributes are intentionally absent from the feature set.
- PSI alerts trigger investigation; they do not silently retrain a model.

Production deployment would require retention controls, deletion workflows, lawful-basis documentation, access controls, encryption, bias testing and approval from privacy, legal, marketing and sales stakeholders.
